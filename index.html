Heatmaps, Scroll depth and flows all tracked inside your prototype. Just add one line of code.

This page is running Recap right now.

Click around a few times, then press Alt+Shift+Y to see the heatmap of your own session.

What it records

Clicks
Positions, selectors, and semantic labels for everything tapped.
Scroll
Depth per page, milestone and continuous.
Navigation
Which pages, in what order, for how long.

How you use it

  1. Add the script tag to your prototype.
  2. Let people click through it as they normally would.
  3. Press Alt+Shift+Y (or hold three fingers on mobile) to open the panel.
  4. Review heatmaps, scroll depth and flows, or export a summary.

Why it's different

Paste the exported summary into Claude, ChatGPT or any coding agent. Recap is built to be read by them.

  • One line to start. No build step, accounts or backend.
  • Runs anywhere. Anywhere a script tag runs: Claude artifacts, Vercel previews, Framer or localhost.
  • Private by design. No cookies. Data stays on the device until you export it.
  • Built for AI. A plain-English session summary, friction included.
  • Open source. MIT licensed. Read it, fork it, run it yourself.
