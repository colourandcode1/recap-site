Testing analytics for your AI prototypes.

Recap is drop-in usability analytics for prototypes built with Claude, Cursor, v0, or any other AI tool. One script tag. No backend. No accounts. Maze, but for the thing AI built you in five minutes.

How it works

Recap is running on this page right now. Click around. Then press Alt+Shift+R.

Click these a few times, then press Alt+Shift+R to see the heatmap:

Clicks
Positions, selectors, and semantic labels for everything tapped.
Scroll
Depth per page, milestone and continuous.
Navigation
Which pages, in what order, for how long.
